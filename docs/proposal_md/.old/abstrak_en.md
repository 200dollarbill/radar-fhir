# Development of a Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR) Profile for the Interoperability of Jugular Vein Pulse-Detecting RADAR Devices

# Abstract

This research develops and validates a Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR) profile compliant with the SATUSEHAT standard for integrating RADAR-based health monitoring devices, using a RADAR device detecting the Jugular Vein Pulse (JVP) as a case study. RADAR devices produce continuous contactless monitoring data in the form of signals and derived features, but no standard representation exists in FHIR R4 and the SATUSEHAT implementation guide, making such output difficult to record in the Electronic Medical Record (EMR) and exchange between systems. The research method begins with identifying the output data elements of the RADAR device and mapping them to FHIR resources along with LOINC and SNOMED CT terminology systems, followed by designing a profile based on the Indonesian Ministry of Health Observation profile and supporting resources comprising Device, DiagnosticReport, and Provenance that represent measurement signals, derived features, interpretation results, and device metadata. The profile is implemented in a full-stack system that processes actual measurement data from the device into FHIR resources, which are validated and presented to three user roles: patients, doctors, and administrators. Validation is carried out using the HL7 FHIR Validator against FHIR R4 and the Ministry of Health profiles, round-trip testing to ensure data integrity, and review of the semantic consistency between the source data and the generated resources. The results show that all resources generated from actual measurement samples pass validation without error, data representation remains intact throughout the processing, storage, retrieval, and presentation pipeline, and the resulting profile provides a consistent mapping reference for recording RADAR monitoring data in the EMR and exchanging it within the SATUSEHAT ecosystem.

**Keywords:** FHIR, SATUSEHAT, interoperability, FHIR profile, RADAR, Jugular Vein Pulse, Electronic Medical Record.
